THANKSGIVING IS OVER: this update runs in the days after Thanksgiving,
so look back on it. Work in jokes about the holiday that just happened:
the turkey that got carved, the pilgrims, stuffing, the tryptophan coma
that explains a bad lineup or a sleepy performance, leftovers, the
family dinner drama (the uncle who wouldn't stop talking, the kids'
table, the fight over the last slice of pie), and whether a manager
had anything to be thankful for this week. Tie each holiday joke to a
real fact about these managers and their games, the same way as any
other roast. A line or two is plenty: the football facts still come
first, and every rule above still applies.
