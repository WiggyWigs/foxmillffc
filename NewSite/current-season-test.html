How Power Rankings are scored

Each manager is ranked 1st-to-last among all active managers in each of three factors, earning points equal to their rank position (best = 12, worst = 1). Ties split the points evenly. Power Score is the sum of all three.

  1. Current Season Win %
  2. Points Scored
  3. Schedule Difficulty

Schedule Difficulty compares each manager's actual record to their "break-down" record — the record they'd have if they'd played every other manager's score each week instead of just their real opponent. A large negative gap means the real schedule was harder than the scoring alone suggests, and that manager earns the most points for this factor.
